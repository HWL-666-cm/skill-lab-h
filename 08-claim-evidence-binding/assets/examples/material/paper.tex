\section{Introduction}
Sea fog severely reduces visibility and disrupts maritime traffic. However, existing
statistical approaches remain limited in complex coastal settings.

\section{Related Work}
\subsection{Problem setting}
Smith et al. (2020) studied visibility prediction with logistic regression.
Lee et al. (2021) used random forests for fog detection.
\subsection{Method routes}
Zhang and Wang (2022) applied a convolutional network to satellite imagery.
Chen et al. (2023) proposed a physics-guided model.

\section{Research Gap}
However, these works do not jointly consider visibility thresholds and weather codes,
and limited attention has been paid to class imbalance.
